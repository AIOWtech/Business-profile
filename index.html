About AIOWtech

AIOWtech is a growing technology brand focused on building modern digital solutions for businesses, students, and startups. From websites to intelligent applications, we help ideas become real, functional, and scalable products.

We specialize in web development, mobile apps, UI/UX design, and interactive systems that improve user experience and business performance.

Our mission is simple — to empower individuals and businesses in Nigeria and beyond with powerful, affordable, and innovative technology.

👨‍💻 Meet the Founder

AIOWtech is founded and owned by Charles Hyellafiya Jeremiah, a passionate software developer and tech entrepreneur based in Nigeria.

Charles specializes in building interactive web applications, AI-powered systems, and smart digital tools. He is driven by a vision to create impactful technology solutions that solve real-world problems.

With projects like an AI assistant (MISS) and interactive quiz platforms, he continues to push boundaries and grow AIOWtech into a recognized tech brand.

His long-term goal is to build AIOWtech into one of the leading tech companies in Nigeria and globally.
